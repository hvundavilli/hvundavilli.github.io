Philosophy

Mentorship

The lab works best as a collaborative, curious place. My job is to help each student become an independent scientist — able to frame a question, build the model or the mooring, analyze the data, and write it up. I tailor projects and professional development to where each person wants to go, whether that's academia, agencies, or industry.

Prospective students

GraduateM.S. / Ph.D.

I recruit students interested in coastal physical oceanography, delta and estuary dynamics, sediment transport, and numerical modeling. Backgrounds in oceanography, geology, environmental science, engineering, physics, or math are all welcome. Email me a CV, a short statement of research interests, and unofficial transcripts.

UndergradResearch

Undergraduates in the lab learn Delft3D modeling, MATLAB/Python data analysis, and coastal fieldwork, with chances to present at conferences. Reach out to talk projects.
